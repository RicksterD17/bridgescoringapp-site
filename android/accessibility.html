Accessibility

Duplicate Bridge Scoring for Android · Version 2.0

Duplicate Bridge Scoring is made to be easy to read and easy to tap. These are the accessibility features it supports, and what each one means in the app.

Larger text

The app follows your phone's font size and has its own Text Size setting so you can make it larger than the rest of your phone or tablet. At the largest sizes, rows of buttons and columns rearrange so nothing is cut off.

Big buttons

Choices are big buttons rather than small menus: the side of the score, the level, suit and declarer of a contract, the result (“Made 4”, “Down 1”, with the score under each), and every board in Go to Board.

TalkBack

Buttons say what they do in words, for example “North-South plus”, “Hearts” or “Made 4, NS +420”, and each table's row on Scores is read together: the pairs, the score and the matchpoints. When Save & Next moves on to a new board, TalkBack says which board and table.

Dark theme

Under Settings → Appearance, choose Light, Dark or the same as your phone or tablet. Every screen works in both.

Contrast and color

The app uses the same colors as the iPhone version, where text meets a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1. Nothing is shown by color alone: vulnerability is written out in words, and on the printouts it is marked with a black “VUL” tag.

Feedback

If something in the app doesn't work with the way you use your phone or tablet, please tell us: bridgescoringapp@gmail.com. ☰ → Settings → Contact Support in the app adds your device details for you.
