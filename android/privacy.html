Privacy Policy

Duplicate Bridge Scoring for Android · Effective November 12, 2026

Duplicate Bridge Scoring is built to need as little information about you as possible. There are no accounts and no ads, and the app has no servers of its own.

Your matches

Your matches, including the pairs' names and the scores, are stored on your phone or tablet and are never sent to us. If Android backup is turned on, they're included in your device's backup to your Google account, under Google's privacy policy. When you print, the app makes the pages on your device and sends them to the printer you choose. When you share a PDF, it goes only where you send it.

Anonymous usage statistics

The app sends small anonymous reports through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service based in Germany, so we can see which features are used. A report says, for example, that the app was opened, that a match was created (with the number of pairs, the movement and the scoring), that a match was completed (with the number of pairs and boards, and how many results were typed, entered by contract, passed out or awarded), which printout was printed or shared, or which text size or appearance was chosen. Reports also include your device type, Android version and language settings, as with most apps, and an anonymous identifier that TelemetryDeck can't link to you.

Reports never include pair or match names, scores, contracts, dates or anything you type, and nothing that identifies you. You can turn them off in Settings → Share anonymous usage statistics.

Contacting support

When you tap Contact Support in Settings, the app starts an email in your email app that already includes the app version, your Android version and your device's maker and model, to help us answer. You can change or delete these details, nothing is sent unless you send the email, and we use your email only to answer you.

Ratings

After you complete a few matches, the app may ask Google Play to show its rating request. Google handles it under Google's privacy policy; the app never sees what you answer.

Contact

Questions about privacy: bridgescoringapp@gmail.com.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
