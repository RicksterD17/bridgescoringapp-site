Run a duplicate bridge game at home, on Android phones and tablets.

Common questions

How do I print table cards and travelers?
Open the match, go to the Movement tab and tap Print. Table cards prints one page for each table. Travelers prints one for each board, four or two to a page, to cut apart, with the pairs already filled in. Enter the pairs' names on the Pairs tab first and they appear on everything you print. You can print to any printer your phone can use, or share a PDF.

How do I make the text bigger?
Tap Settings at the top of the screen. Under Text Size, use the slider or the A buttons. Use Phone Setting (or Tablet) goes back to your device's own font size.

How do I enter a score?
On the Scores tab, tap a table. Choose NS + or EW + and type the score as it's written on the traveler, or tap Contract and tap the contract and the result. Save & Next Table goes straight on to the next table.

Are my matches backed up?
Your matches are stored on your phone or tablet. If Android backup is turned on, they're included in your device's backup and come back when you set up a new phone from it.

I marked a match as completed by mistake.
Open it and tap Reopen Match at the bottom of Scores, Pairs or Results.
