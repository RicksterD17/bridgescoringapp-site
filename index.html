Duplicate Bridge Scoring

Run a duplicate bridge game at home.

For iPhone, iPad and Android. Easy to read, with big buttons and large text.

Table cards and travelers for tonight's game

Enter the pairs' names, and the app prints a card for each table and a traveler for each board, with tonight's pairs already filled in. Put the cards on the tables, cut the travelers apart, and play.

The right movement

Howell and Mitchell movements for 4 to 36 pairs. With an odd number of pairs, the app sits a pair out for you.

Scores in, results out

Type each score as it's written on the traveler, or tap the contract. Save & Next goes straight to the next table.

Easy to read

Big buttons, a Text Size setting that goes very large, and light or dark appearance.

No account, no ads

Your matches stay on your device. One price, no subscription.
