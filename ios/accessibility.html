Accessibility

Duplicate Bridge Scoring for iPhone and iPad · Version 2.0

Duplicate Bridge Scoring is made to be easy to read and easy to tap, and is built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA on iPhone and iPad. These are the accessibility features it supports, and what each one means in the app.

Larger Text

The app follows your text size, up to the largest accessibility sizes, and has its own Text Size setting so you can make it larger than the rest of your iPhone or iPad. At the largest sizes, rows of buttons and columns rearrange so nothing is cut off.

Big buttons

Choices are big buttons rather than small menus: the side of the score, the level, suit and declarer of a contract, the result (“Made 4”, “Down 1”, with the score under each), and every board in Go to Board.

VoiceOver

Every screen works with VoiceOver. Buttons say what they do in words, for example “North-South plus”, “Hearts” or “Made 4, NS +420”, and each table's result is read as one sentence. When Save & Next moves on to a new board, VoiceOver says which board and table.

Dark Interface

Under Settings → Appearance, choose Light, Dark or the same as your iPhone or iPad. Every screen works in both.

Sufficient Contrast

Text meets a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 in light and dark appearance, including the red suits and the gray notes.

Differentiate Without Color

Nothing is shown by color alone. Vulnerability is written out in words, and on the printouts it is marked with a black “VUL” tag.

Reduced Motion

With Reduce Motion on, the board menu's arrow and other changes happen instantly, without animation.

Feedback

If something in the app doesn't work with the way you use your iPhone or iPad, please tell us: bridgescoringapp@gmail.com. Settings → Contact Support in the app adds your device details for you.
