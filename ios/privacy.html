Privacy Policy

Duplicate Bridge Scoring for iPhone and iPad · Effective November 12, 2026

Duplicate Bridge Scoring is built to need as little information about you as possible. There are no accounts and no ads, and the app has no servers of its own.

Your matches

Your matches, including the pairs' names and the scores, are stored on your iPhone or iPad and are never sent to us. Like other app data, they're included in your device's backups. When you print, the app makes the pages on your device and sends them to the printer you choose. When you share a PDF, it goes only where you send it.

Anonymous usage statistics

The app sends small anonymous reports through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service based in Germany, so we can see which features are used. A report says, for example, that the app was opened, that a match was created (with the number of pairs, the movement and the scoring), that a match was completed (with the number of pairs and boards, and how many results were typed, entered by contract, passed out or awarded), which printout was printed or shared, or which text size or appearance was chosen. Reports also include your device type, system version and language settings, as with most apps, and an anonymous identifier that TelemetryDeck can't link to you.

Reports never include pair or match names, scores, contracts, dates or anything you type, and nothing that identifies you. You can turn them off in Settings → Share anonymous usage statistics.

Upgrading from version 1.2

When you first open 2.0, your old matches are converted on your device. If some can't be converted, the app offers to start an email to us with details about those matches and a copy of the old match file with the pairs' names removed, so we can bring them over for you. The email is shown to you first and is sent only if you send it.

Contacting support

When you tap Contact Support in Settings, the app starts an email that already includes the app version, your iOS version and your device model, to help us answer. You can change or delete these details, nothing is sent unless you send the email, and we use your email only to answer you.

Ratings

After you complete a few matches, the app may ask iOS to show Apple's rating request. Apple handles it under Apple's privacy policy; the app never sees what you answer.

Contact

Questions about privacy: bridgescoringapp@gmail.com.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
