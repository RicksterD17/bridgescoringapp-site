Common questions

How do I print table cards and travelers?
Open the match, go to the Movement tab and tap Print. Table cards prints one page for each table. Travelers prints one for each board, four or two to a page, to cut apart, with the pairs already filled in. Enter the pairs' names on the Pairs tab first and they appear on everything you print. You can print to an AirPrint printer or share a PDF.

I'm upgrading from version 1.2. Where are my matches?
Your saved matches come over to 2.0 by themselves the first time you open it, and appear in the list, newest first. If any can't be brought over, the app tells you and offers to send the details to us so we can help.

Where did the tabs and SP codes from version 1.2 go?
Team Names is now Pairs, Table Movement is now Movement and Input Scores is now Scores. The special scores are buttons now: R is the Sat the wrong way switch; A, T and B are Other → Director award; O is automatic (with an odd number of pairs, the app sits a pair out for you). In the app, Settings → Help has the same list under “Coming from version 1.2”.

How do I make the text bigger?
Tap Settings at the top of the screen. Under Text Size, use the slider or the A buttons. Use iPhone Setting (or iPad) goes back to your device's own size.

How do I enter a score?
On the Scores tab, tap a table. Choose NS + or EW + and type the score as it's written on the traveler, or tap Contract and tap the contract and the result. Save & Next Table goes straight on to the next table.

I marked a match as completed by mistake.
Open it and tap Reopen Match at the bottom of Scores, Pairs or Results.
